# The Hand and the Glove

*A mão e a luva*, by Machado de Assis (1874) — translated into English by Josué B. Almeida.

A faithful English translation of Machado de Assis's novel, complete in 19 chapters, with nothing added or left out.

## Credits and rights

- **Author:** Machado de Assis, *A mão e a luva* (1874).
- **Original text:** in the public domain (the author died in 1908). The translation is by Josué B. Almeida.
- **Text compared:** the Portuguese edition on Wikisource, <https://pt.wikisource.org/wiki/A_m%C3%A3o_e_a_luva>, used to mend the damage the OCR left in the source file (chapters I and II and the chapter-opening dashes) and to settle "crua cor" (ch. IV) and "confissão oral" (ch. VIII).
- **Source file:** the Portuguese original kept in [`original/`](original/) (`01-intro.md` to `20-xix-conclusao.md`).
