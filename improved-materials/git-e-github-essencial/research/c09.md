## Fatos a confirmar ao escrever (fontes oficiais listadas em 2026-11-21)

- Convidar colaborador num repositório pessoal: Settings → Collaborators. https://docs.github.com/pt/account-and-profile/setting-up-and-managing-your-personal-account-on-github/managing-access-to-your-personal-repositories/inviting-collaborators-to-a-personal-repository

- Métodos de merge do PR: merge commit, squash, rebase. https://docs.github.com/pt/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/configuring-pull-request-merges/about-merge-methods-on-github
