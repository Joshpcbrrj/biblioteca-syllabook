# Dossiê c06: Contando o que aconteceu

Meta: contar a última viagem ou semana de trabalho em 8 frases no passado. CEFR A2.

## Fatos estáveis
- Passado simples regular: *-ed* (pronúncia /t/, /d/, /ɪd/: *worked, played, wanted*); ortografia (*study → studied, stop → stopped*).
- Irregulares de alta frequência (lista para ensinar, ~30): be (was/were), have (had), do (did), go (went), see (saw), take (took), make (made), get (got), come (came), give (gave), say (said), know (knew), think (thought), buy (bought), send (sent), write (wrote), read (read /red/), meet (met), leave (left), find (found), tell (told), feel (felt), begin (began), eat (ate), drink (drank), fly (flew), run (ran), pay (paid), lose (lost). Verificar cada forma antes de publicar.
- Perguntas e negativas: *did* + verbo base (*Did you go...? I didn't go*): o verbo principal volta ao infinitivo.
- Conectores de sequência: first, then, after that, later, finally, in the end; marcadores de tempo: yesterday, last week, two days ago, in 2024.

## Erros comuns de brasileiros
- *Did you went*; *I have been in Paris last year* (present perfect com marcador de passado: ver cap. 9); epêntese em *stopped*, *asked* (não "askidi").
